Books and Reading

Read widely across fiction and nonfiction to cross-pollinate ideas. Novels build empathy; essays sharpen argument.

The 50-page rule: if a book has not hooked you by page 50, it is fine to quit and pick another.

Annotate sparingly. One margin note per chapter forces you to choose what actually mattered.

Library cards still unlock free ebooks and audiobooks through apps like Libby in many regions.

Classic starters: 1984 for dystopia, Pride and Prejudice for wit and social observation, Dune for epic world-building.

Short story collections fit busy weeks. One story per night finishes a book without pressure.

Re-read favorites every few years. You change; the same pages reveal new lines.

Reading before screens at night can improve sleep compared with scrolling in bright blue light.
