Fitness and Health

Walk 30 minutes most days. It is the most underrated exercise for heart health and mood.

Strength training twice a week preserves muscle and bone density as you age. Bodyweight squats and push-ups count.

Hydration before thirst on hot days prevents headaches. Urine pale yellow is a simple rough guide.

Stretch hips and shoulders if you sit long hours. Two minutes between meetings adds up.

Protein at each meal supports recovery after workouts. Eggs, beans, yogurt, and fish are easy options.

Rest days are training days for adaptation. Muscles grow during recovery, not during the lift itself.

Sleep seven to nine hours when possible. Consistent wake times beat random long weekend catch-up.
