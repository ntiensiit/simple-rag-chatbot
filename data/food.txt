Food Around the World

Neapolitan pizza uses San Marzano tomatoes, mozzarella di bufala, and a blistered thin crust baked at very high heat.

Japanese umami comes from dashi, miso, soy sauce, and kombu. Umami was identified as a fifth basic taste in the early 1900s.

Ethiopian injera is a spongy sour flatbread made from teff flour. Meals are shared from one large platter.

Peruvian ceviche cures raw fish in lime juice with ají chili, onion, and cilantro. Freshness and acidity matter most.

Indian thali serves many small dishes on one plate: dal, vegetables, rice, bread, pickle, and dessert depending on region.

Mexican mole can contain dozens of ingredients including chili, chocolate, nuts, and spices, simmered for hours.

Vietnamese pho broth simmers beef bones with star anise, cinnamon, cloves, and charred onion for depth.
