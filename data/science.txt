Science Curiosities

Honey never spoils in sealed jars. Archaeologists have found edible honey in ancient Egyptian tombs.

Octopuses have three hearts and blue blood. Two hearts pump to the gills; one pumps to the rest of the body.

Bananas are berries botanically. Strawberries are not berries; they are aggregate fruits.

Lightning is hotter than the surface of the sun for a brief instant along the bolt channel.

Your brain uses about 20 percent of your body's energy despite being roughly 2 percent of body weight.

Venus rotates backward compared with most planets. A Venusian day is longer than its year.

Trees communicate through underground fungal networks sometimes called the wood wide web.

The speed of sound is about 343 meters per second in dry air at room temperature.
