Travel Tips and Destinations

Pack light and leave room for things you find on the road. One carry-on teaches you what you actually need.

Learn five local phrases: hello, please, thank you, excuse me, and how much. Locals notice the effort.

Travel off-peak for lower prices and fewer crowds. Shoulder season often has good weather without peak rates.

Photocopy or save digital copies of passport and insurance separately from the originals.

Kyoto is famous for temples, bamboo groves, and seasonal cherry blossoms. Early morning visits avoid tour groups.

Iceland's Ring Road circles the island past waterfalls, glaciers, and black-sand beaches. Rent a car and allow at least a week.

Marrakech mixes spice markets, riads, and the Atlas Mountains nearby. Bargaining is normal in the souks.

Lisbon climbs seven hills with trams, fado music, and pastel de nata custard tarts by the waterfront.

Slow travel: stay longer in fewer places instead of rushing a checklist of landmarks.
