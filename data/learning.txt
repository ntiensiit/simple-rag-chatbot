Learning How to Learn

Spaced repetition beats cramming. Review material at increasing intervals: 1 day, 3 days, 1 week, 2 weeks.

The Feynman technique: explain a concept in simple words as if teaching a beginner. Gaps in your explanation show what to study next.

Active recall is stronger than re-reading. Close the book and write what you remember before checking notes.

Interleaving mixes related topics in one study session instead of blocking one subject for hours.

Sleep consolidates memory. A short review before bed helps retention more than another late-night hour of reading.

Teach someone else, even aloud to yourself. Production forces deeper processing than passive input.
